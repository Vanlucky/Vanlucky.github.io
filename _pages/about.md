  <p>Student</p>

  <p>I am currently pursuing my Master's degree at Academy of Military Science. You can view my personal information on my <a href="/cv">CV</a>, and my research papers are available at <a href="/publications">Publications</a>. For further inquiries, feel free to contact me via WeChat or phone.</p>

  <p>I am the president of the Harbin Engineering University (HEU) Symphony Orchestra. As an individual, I have performed at major university events such as the Opening Ceremony for the Class of 2023 undergraduates and the 70th Anniversary Innovation and Development Conference. I have been featured 15 times on the HEU official WeChat account. During my tenure as president, I organized the orchestra's special concert and led the group to participate in numerous key art and aesthetic education events at HEU, the highest number among all university art troupes. The Military Band performed on CCTV-7's National Defense and Military Channel, and the Symphony Orchestra was reported on by national media outlets such as China National Radio Online (CNR). These efforts have made significant contributions to the university's aesthetic education initiatives.</p>

  <p>During my tenure as a student assistant, I actively fulfilled my duties and participated in various university-level and provincial projects. I served as a staff member for the Heilongjiang Provincial Demonstration Training Program for Young Teachers, assisted in preliminary evaluations for provincial and national Distinguished Teaching Awards, and supported the undergraduate education assessment and the China Higher Education Expo.</p>

  <p>I assisted in organizing more than twenty faculty training workshops, as well as major university events such as the 2nd National High School Principals Forum and the Rectification Deployment Meeting for Undergraduate Education Evaluation.</p>

  <p>I also coordinated media reception tasks, including serving with CCTV and Heilongjiang provincial news outlets, and contributed to report submissions to the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology.</p>

  <p>In important university meetings, such as the Symposium on Studying and Implementing President Xi Jinping's Important Instructions during his visit to HEU and the "Smart Agriculture + Low-Altitude Industry" Innovation Development Promotion Conference, I assist teachers in preparing and recording meeting materials.</p>
